Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy for Sruthi Kondiparthy, Einzelunternehmerin, doing business as Athirikya (“we”, “us”, or “our”) describes how and why we may access, collect, store, use, and/or share (“process”) personal information when you use our services (“Services”), including when you download or use the MyGermanFreund mobile application.

MyGermanFreund helps users understand German letters, prepare for everyday conversations, and discover practical information about life in Germany.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Services. If you have any questions or concerns, please use our Contact page.

Summary of Key Points

What personal information do we process?

When you use MyGermanFreund, we may process information depending on the features you use, such as scanned-letter text, app usage information, anonymous authentication identifiers, and technical service logs.

Do we process sensitive personal information?

Letters scanned by users may contain sensitive information, such as health, financial, student, government, or official reference information. MyGermanFreund applies best-effort redaction before AI-assisted analysis, but automated redaction may not remove all personal or sensitive information.

Do we collect information from third parties?

We do not collect personal information about you from third parties.

How do we process your information?

We process information to provide the requested app features, operate and secure the Services, manage scan limits, troubleshoot issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

With whom do we share information?

We may share information with service providers required to operate MyGermanFreund, including cloud, authentication, AI processing, and infrastructure providers. User-triggered sharing or calendar actions may send information to external apps or services selected by the user.

Do we use analytics or advertising?

The current version of MyGermanFreund does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party advertising services.

How long do we keep information?

Uploaded letters, extracted letter text, and generated summaries are not permanently stored by MyGermanFreund as part of the normal current-version letter-analysis flow. Basic usage records and technical logs may be retained as needed to operate, monitor, secure, and protect the service.

What are your rights?

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws. You may contact us through the Contact page with privacy-related questions.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us or use features of the Services.

The current version of MyGermanFreund does not require visible user registration, email/password login, a user profile, or direct account creation by the user. The app may use anonymous authentication identifiers to operate app functionality and manage monthly scan limits.

Sensitive information

When you use the Letter Scanner, the letters you scan may contain sensitive information. Depending on the document, this may include health data, financial data, student data, government identifiers, reference numbers, official case numbers, payment information, or other personal details.

We process such information only as needed to provide the requested letter explanation feature, with best-effort redaction before AI-assisted analysis. Automated redaction may not remove all personal or sensitive information.

Application data

If you use the MyGermanFreund mobile application, we may request access or permission to certain features on your device, including:

  • camera access;
  • storage or document/image selection;
  • Android share functionality selected by the user;
  • user-triggered calendar actions.

You can change app permissions through your device settings.

This information is primarily needed to provide app functionality, maintain the security and operation of the application, manage scan limits, troubleshoot issues, and monitor service reliability.

Information automatically collected

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the Services. This information may include service-related, diagnostic, usage, and performance information, such as timestamps, request paths, request status codes, request duration, authentication status, and technical service metadata.

This information is primarily needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and protect the Services.

The current version of MyGermanFreund does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party advertising tracking.

Google API services

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, where applicable.

We provide AI-assisted functionality through third-party AI service providers, including Anthropic. Your input, output, and related information may be shared with and processed by these providers only as needed to provide the requested AI-assisted feature.

Our AI-assisted features are designed for:

When you use the Letter Scanner, MyGermanFreund processes text extracted from the letter in order to generate the explanation requested by you.

Letters may contain personal or sensitive information. MyGermanFreund applies best-effort redaction before AI-assisted analysis, but automated redaction may not remove all personal or sensitive information.

MyGermanFreund does not permanently store uploaded letters, extracted letter text, or generated summaries as part of the normal letter-analysis process.

You remain responsible for checking the original document, verifying deadlines, confirming payment details, and deciding what action to take.
