Advent in Germany: Wreaths, Calendars and Traditions

Understand the four Sundays of Advent, wreaths, calendars and simple ways to enjoy the season.

What Advent means

Advent is the period before Christmas. In Christian tradition it is a time of preparation and anticipation. Today, people in Germany observe it in different religious, cultural and personal ways; participation is a choice.

Advent begins on the fourth Sunday before Christmas. Its starting date changes each year and may fall in November.

The Advent wreath

Many homes, schools and churches display an Adventskranz, traditionally made with evergreen branches and four candles. One candle is lit on the first Advent Sunday, with another added each Sunday.

If you use real candles, place the wreath on a stable, heat-resistant surface and never leave flames unattended. LED candles are an alternative.

Advent calendars and everyday traditions

Many Advent calendars have 24 doors or packages opened from 1 to 24 December. That is different from the beginning of Advent, which follows the four Sundays before Christmas. Calendars can contain pictures, chocolate or small surprises; some families make their own.

People may also bake Plätzchen, attend concerts or visit a Christmas market. Customs vary between families and regions, and there is no obligation to take part.

What to avoid

  • Do not assume all families celebrate Advent in the same way.
  • Do not leave burning candles unattended, particularly near dry branches or decorations.
  • Do not assume Advent calendars always start on the first Sunday of Advent.

When to ask

If a school or workplace plans an activity, ask the organiser about the date, any contribution requested and whether participation is optional. Ask about ingredients if you have allergies or dietary needs.

Useful German words

  • die Adventszeit — Advent season
  • der Adventskranz — Advent wreath
  • die Kerze — candle
  • das Türchen — little door
  • die Plätzchen — Christmas biscuits

Wann zündet ihr die erste Kerze an? — When do you light the first candle?
