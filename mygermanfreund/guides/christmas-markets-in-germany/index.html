Christmas Markets in Germany: Plan Your First Visit

A practical introduction to finding a market, enjoying the atmosphere and planning a relaxed visit as a newcomer.

What a Christmas market is

A Weihnachtsmarkt is a seasonal outdoor market, usually held in town squares or historic centres from November through December. You will find small stalls with warm drinks, food, gifts, crafts and seasonal decorations. Some cities call their market a Christkindlmarkt or Adventsmarkt.

Markets differ greatly in size and style. A small neighbourhood market may run for one weekend, while a city-centre market can run for several weeks.

Check the local details before you go

Do not rely on a national list for the exact date or opening time. Each town sets its own programme. Look for the official city, tourism or event-organiser website and check the location, dates, opening hours and travel information.

Some markets open in mid-November, while others start later. Sundays can also be different: certain places may close or have shorter hours on a quiet Sunday such as Totensonntag. The local organiser is the reliable source.

Food, drinks and deposits

Typical food includes Bratwurst, potato dishes, roasted chestnuts, sweets and regional specialities. You may also find vegetarian or vegan options, but the choice varies by stall. Ask directly if you need information about ingredients or allergens.

Warm drinks are often served in reusable cups. You may pay a refundable deposit, called Pfand, when you receive the cup. Keep the cup and return it to the correct stall or return point when you leave.

Payment and a comfortable visit

Payment options differ from stall to stall. Some accept cards, while others may accept cash only. Taking a small amount of cash and a payment card is practical.

Markets can become busy in the evenings and at weekends. If you prefer a quieter visit, go earlier in the day or on a weekday. Dress warmly and wear comfortable shoes, especially if you plan to walk around for a while.

What to avoid

  • Do not assume every market starts on the same date or has the same opening hours.
  • Do not assume every stall accepts card payments.
  • Do not throw away a reusable cup before checking whether it has a deposit.
  • Do not guess about ingredients or allergens; ask the stall directly.
  • Do not rely on a social-media post for last-minute changes; check the official organiser’s information.

When to ask

Ask the organiser or city information point when you need current opening hours, accessibility information, a lost-child meeting point or the location of toilets. Ask a stall directly about allergens, vegetarian food, alcohol or payment methods.

Useful German words

  • der Weihnachtsmarkt — Christmas market
  • der Stand — stall
  • die Öffnungszeiten — opening hours
  • das Pfand — refundable deposit
  • der Becher — cup
  • bar zahlen — to pay in cash
  • die Zutaten — ingredients

Useful questions

Kann ich mit Karte zahlen? — Can I pay by card?

Wo kann ich den Becher zurückgeben? — Where can I return the cup?
