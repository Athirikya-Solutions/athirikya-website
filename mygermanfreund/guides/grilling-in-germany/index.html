Grilling in Germany: Where Is It Allowed?

How to check local rules and enjoy grilling responsibly in Germany.

Why grilling rules vary by location

Grilling is popular in Germany during summer, but you cannot assume that it is allowed in every park or public green space. Rules can differ between cities and locations, and temporary restrictions can apply during hot and dry weather.

Where can I grill in a park?

Some cities provide designated areas called Grillplätze. Other parks or green spaces may prohibit grilling. There is no simple Germany-wide rule that means grilling is allowed in every park.

Before going, search for Grillplatz + city name or Grillen + city name. Prefer the official website of the relevant Stadt or Gemeinde, and also check the notices and rules displayed at the Grillplatz or park.

Before you light the grill

  • Check whether grilling is permitted at the location.
  • Check whether you must use a designated Grillplatz.
  • Check for temporary fire or weather-related restrictions.
  • Follow the rules displayed at the location.
  • Dispose of hot charcoal, ash and rubbish safely.

Example: Frankfurt am Main

Frankfurt is a concrete local example, not a Germany-wide rule. Under normal conditions, Frankfurt permits grilling in public green spaces only at specifically designated Grillplätze.

Current example: August 2026

As of August 2026, the Stadt Frankfurt am Main says grilling with open fire is temporarily prohibited on all municipal public Grillplätze because of dry conditions and increased fire risk. The prohibition has applied since 24 June 2026 and remains in force until the Grünflächenamt actively lifts it.

The prohibition is monitored by the Ordnungsamt.

This is exactly why you should check current local information before leaving home.

Can I grill on my balcony?

Grilling on a balcony is not automatically prohibited everywhere. However, an explicit prohibition in the Mietvertrag can prohibit grilling, so also check your Hausordnung.

Even without an explicit contractual prohibition, grilling can be unacceptable when smoke enters neighbouring apartments. Consider your neighbours and do not treat balcony grilling as a universal legal entitlement.

What about a garden or terrace?

Private space may provide more flexibility, but you still need to consider fire safety, smoke, neighbours, and any rental or property rules that apply. The details depend on your situation, so avoid assuming that one broad rule covers every private garden or terrace.

Can I grill inside my apartment?

An electric tabletop grill designed for indoor cooking is different from a charcoal grill or another outdoor grill that burns fuel. Use indoor cooking equipment only as directed by its manufacturer.

Safety notice

Never use a charcoal grill or another outdoor combustion grill indoors.

Burning charcoal can produce dangerous carbon monoxide, which can build up without being seen or smelled.

Check the rules again on the day

Finding a Grillplatz does not necessarily mean you can grill there today.

Temporary restrictions can be introduced because of drought, heat or fire risk. Check current information from the relevant Stadt, Gemeinde, or responsible authority before going.

Useful German words

  • der Grillplatz — designated barbecue area
  • grillen — to barbecue / grill
  • erlaubt — permitted
  • verboten — prohibited
  • die Hausordnung — house rules
  • der Mietvertrag — rental agreement
  • das Grünflächenamt — the German authority name commonly used for the municipal office responsible for public green spaces
  • das Ordnungsamt — the German authority name for a municipal office that monitors compliance with local public-order rules
