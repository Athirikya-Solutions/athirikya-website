# Athirikya Content Strategy

## Core principle

**Real life comes first. Structure comes second.**

German Buzz reflects what people in Germany are actually experiencing during a given week. Weekly topics must not be selected to fill a category or satisfy a predefined taxonomy.

## First-year learning phase

The first year is intentionally exploratory for Athirikya.

- German Buzz is the primary source of new observations.
- Life Areas provide a broad, expandable backbone.
- Knowledge Units remain empty until real recurring experiences justify them.
- New Knowledge Units may be created, merged, renamed or moved as understanding improves.
- New Life Areas may be added if real topics repeatedly reveal a gap.

## Editorial order

1. Identify four real, timely German Buzz topics.
2. Publish the weekly learning and conversation content.
3. Review what those experiences teach Athirikya.
4. Place each experience under the most suitable existing Life Area.
5. Create or enrich a Knowledge Unit only when the weekly topic genuinely supports it.

The Life Area structure follows German Buzz. German Buzz does not follow the Life Area structure.

## Website behaviour

Life Area pages are generated from data and are not added to the main website navigation initially. They become reachable through carefully selected `Mehr dazu` links from German Buzz and, later, through other MyGermanFreund features.

The goal is not to create endless standalone pages. Each Life Area page should gradually become richer as real experiences accumulate.
